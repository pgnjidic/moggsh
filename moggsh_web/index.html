// The vibe coding interface

Your server.
Your AI.
Your phone.

Vibe coding means long sessions — you and an AI, back and forth, building something real. Claude generates code on your laptop. You SSH in from your phone to run it, check output, tweak, repeat.

moggsh is built for exactly this loop. The joystick keeps navigation instant. The mic keeps your hands free. tmux means nothing gets lost when you switch apps.

  • Joystick controls for arrow keys, Esc, Tab without switching keyboards
  • Mic button for voice commands when you're too lazy to type
  • tmux detection reattaches your session in one tap
  • Background service keeps SSH alive when you switch apps
Why moggsh

ERROR: session.lost
SSH drops and your running process is gone
FIXED: keep-alive + tmux

Foreground service keeps SSH alive. On reconnect, tmux detection offers one-tap session reattach. Nothing lost.

ERROR: ux.friction
Constant keyboard switching to hit Esc, Tab, arrow keys
FIXED: joystick bar

Permanent d-pad, Esc, Tab, Ctrl+C row — always visible, never interrupts typing flow. Navigate vim, tmux, htop with one thumb.

ERROR: key.management
Storing SSH private keys on Android feels sketchy
FIXED: biometric keystore

Keys encrypted with Android Keystore. Fingerprint or face unlock required to use them. Nothing stored in plaintext, nothing on clipboard.
